How we priced a clinical-trials SaaS: we tested 3 models on 14 customers.

Per-seat lost us two deals because study coordinators share logins. Per-study was easy to sell but swung revenue 4x quarter to quarter. Per-site won: revenue tracks the work, and procurement already budgets per site.

If you sell into regulated research, start with per-site. Our pricing sheet is linked in the comments.
